THE GAP

Satellite data is abundant.
Usable answers are not.

Many Earth-observation workflows still make users discover datasets, understand sensors, choose algorithms, preprocess imagery, compare dates, inspect outputs and interpret the result as separate steps. SatQuery is designed to connect those steps around the user's actual question and selected geography.

WHAT CHANGES

From tool-driven analysis
to question-driven intelligence.

We do not need to name other platforms to explain the difference. The distinction is in how the workflow behaves.
